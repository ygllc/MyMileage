# Delete your data

We take your privacy seriously. This page explains how you can request deletion of any data you have provided in our apps:

1\. **MyMileage**\
MyMileage may store the following data when you use the app:

* Vehicle profiles (name, make, model, year, fuel type, registration number)
* Trip logs (start/end mileage, fuel usage, calculated fuel efficiency)
* Your Google account display name and email (if you sign in with Google)

If you request deletion, we will:

* Permanently remove all vehicle profiles and trip logs from our systems
* Remove your Google account information from our authentication service
* Complete deletion within **7 business days** of your request
