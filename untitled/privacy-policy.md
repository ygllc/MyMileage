# Privacy Policy

#### **1. Introduction**

MyMileage (“we”, “our”, “us”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard the information you provide when using our app.

By using MyMileage, you agree to the terms outlined in this policy.

***

#### **2. Information We Collect**

**a) Personal Information**

When you sign in with Google, we collect your:

* **Name** (Google account display name)
* **Email address**

These are used for account management, authentication, and to display your account information within the app.

**b) Other User-Generated Content**

When you use MyMileage, you may enter:

* Vehicle details (name, make, model, year, fuel type, registration number)
* Trip data (start/end mileage, fuel used, calculated fuel efficiency)

This information is stored locally on your device and is required for the app to function.

**c) User-Generated Files/Documents&#x20;**_**(Optional)**_

If you enable Google Drive sync, your vehicle and trip data will be stored in your Google Drive account. This is considered **first-party storage** with Google, and we do not share your data with any other third parties.

***

#### **3. How We Use Your Information**

We use your data solely for:

* App functionality (vehicle and trip tracking)
* Account management and authentication (Google Sign-In)
* Optional cloud backup and sync (Google Drive)

We **do not** use your data for analytics, advertising, marketing, or sale to third parties.

***

#### **4. Data Sharing**

We do not share your data with third parties. Your information may be transferred to Google for authentication or Google Drive sync — both considered first-party services.

***

#### **5. Data Security**

We use secure transmission (HTTPS) for all data transfers. Data stored in Google Drive is secured by Google’s encryption and access controls.

***

#### **6. Data Retention and Deletion**

We do not retain your personal data beyond what is necessary for providing the app’s functionality.\
You may request deletion of your data at any time by following the instructions here:\
[https://ygllc1.wordpress.com/remove-your-data/](https://ygllc1.wordpress.com/remove-your-data/)

***

#### **7. Children’s Privacy**

MyMileage is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

***

#### **8. Changes to This Privacy Policy**

We may update this policy from time to time. Updates will be posted here with the “Last updated” date.

***
